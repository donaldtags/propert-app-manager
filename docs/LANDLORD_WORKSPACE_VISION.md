# PrimeNest Landlord Workspace — "Property Business Hub"

Product specification for the landlord-facing side of PrimeNest, captured from the founding
master prompt so it persists across sessions. This is the destination, not a sprint backlog —
see the relevant phase plan (created per build) for what's actually buildable against the
current codebase at any given time.

## Positioning

PrimeNest is not a property listing website — it is the operating system for real estate in
Zimbabwe, Africa, and eventually the world. The landlord workspace is not "another landlord
dashboard." It is meant to replace spreadsheets, WhatsApp, notebooks, paper leases, manual rent
collection, scattered maintenance records, and disconnected property management software — the
place where a landlord runs their entire business.

**Naming decision:** call it the **Property Business Hub**, not "Landlord Dashboard." The
framing matters: someone with one house should feel like they're building a property business;
someone with twenty apartments should feel like they have enterprise software; someone with five
hundred units should never feel they've outgrown the platform. This is meant to be one of
PrimeNest's signature positioning moves.

Every feature must answer: "what real problem does this solve?" Don't add a feature because a
competitor has it — only because it saves time, reduces fraud, increases revenue, improves
tenant relationships, automates repetitive work, or produces real business intelligence. Every
screen must answer: what decision can the landlord make faster because of this page, what manual
work does it eliminate, what value does it add, what future business opportunity does it unlock.
The experience should be premium, intelligent, proactive, trustworthy, and scalable — the app a
landlord opens every morning before email, spreadsheets, WhatsApp, or their banking app.

## Home Dashboard

On login, the landlord should immediately understand the health of their business. Executive KPI
cards: total portfolio value, number of properties, number of units, occupied/vacant units,
monthly rental income, expected monthly income, outstanding rent, escrow balance, total expenses,
net profit, occupancy rate, tenant satisfaction, trust score. Plus: revenue charts, occupancy
charts, cash flow, recent activity, notifications, upcoming lease expiries/inspections/
maintenance, a calendar, quick actions, and AI recommendations.

## My Property Portfolio

Properties displayed as business assets, not listings. Each card: photo, name, location, units,
occupied/vacant units, monthly income, expenses, net profit, trust score, property score,
maintenance status, inspection status, escrow status, property manager. Actions: open property,
view analytics, create lease, add tenant, book inspection, generate report.

## Property Operations Center

Every property gets its own operating dashboard: overview, documents, tenants, applications,
maintenance, income, expenses, leases, escrows, insurance, inspections, timeline, analytics,
property passport, trust score, valuation, history, nearby market data, investment potential.

## Property Passport

A permanent digital identity that stays with the property for its lifetime: property ID,
address, coordinates, ownership history, lease history, maintenance history, inspection reports,
insurance, renovations, utility information, valuation history, photos over time, energy/solar
information, internet availability, water source, legal documents, full property timeline.

## Tenant Management

Every tenant gets a professional profile: photo, trust score, verification status, payment
history, lease history, maintenance requests, reviews, documents, messages, escrow history,
applications, occupation, employer (only if voluntarily provided and appropriate), emergency
contact. Actions: message, renew lease, terminate lease, generate statement, download lease, view
history.

## Application Management

Never show applications in arbitrary order — rank applicants using AI-assisted recommendations
based on available verified information: verification status, trust score, income/employment
verification status, rental history, payment reliability, references, application completeness,
risk indicators. Every recommendation must come with an explanation; the landlord always makes
the final call.

## Digital Lease Center

Generate leases with electronic signatures, version history, renewals, extensions, termination,
digital archive, document storage, automatic reminders, renewal suggestions, compliance tracking.

## Rent Collection

Expected vs. collected vs. outstanding rent, late payments, upcoming payments, receipts,
statements, exports, payment analytics, cash flow. Actions: send reminder, generate receipt,
download statement, start escrow.

## Escrow Management

Full lifecycle: pending, funded, released, disputed, refunded — with timeline, documents,
evidence, messages, payment history, AI risk analysis.

## Maintenance Management

Tenants submit requests. Workflow: tenant reports issue → AI categorizes issue → landlord
reviews → assign contractor → track progress → upload photos → tenant confirms completion →
release payment. Track priority, estimated cost, assigned vendor, completion time, photos,
invoices, warranty, history.

## Vendor Marketplace

A network of verified professionals — electricians, plumbers, builders, painters, roofers, solar
installers, ISPs, furniture suppliers, movers, cleaners, security companies, garden services.
Every vendor: verification, trust score, ratings, completed jobs, average response time, pricing,
insurance status, availability, performance analytics. PrimeNest may take a referral/service fee
where appropriate.

## Financial Center

Income, expenses, profit, cash flow, ROI, property and portfolio performance, export reports,
tax summaries, forecasting, budget planning.

## AI Business Assistant

Proactively surfaces: units vacant too long, suggested rent adjustments, market trends, lease
renewals, upcoming inspections, maintenance predictions, expense anomalies, tenant communication
reminders, investment opportunities, portfolio growth suggestions. Every recommendation explains
its reasoning.

## Growth Center

Helps landlords expand: new investment opportunities, high-demand suburbs, rental trends, yield
comparisons, portfolio diversification, potential acquisitions.

## Document Vault

Title deeds, leases, insurance, inspection reports, valuations, receipts, invoices, contracts,
maintenance documents, photos — versioned, with controlled access.

## Communication Center

Unified: messages, announcements, maintenance conversations, lease conversations, escrow
conversations, support tickets, document sharing, read receipts, notifications.

## Automation Center

Landlord-authored automations: rent reminders, lease renewal workflows, inspection scheduling,
maintenance follow-ups, escrow notifications, vacancy alerts, monthly report generation, annual
property reviews.

## Analytics

Revenue trends, occupancy trends, maintenance costs, tenant retention, vacancy analysis, rental
yield, profitability, market comparisons, portfolio performance, forecasts.
